Questions about voting

Who can vote?

Active IEEE members of the India Council as of 31 March 2026, at Member, Senior Member, Life Member or Fellow grade.

I didn’t get a ballot email.

Check spam, promotions and any older email address on your IEEE profile. If it still isn’t there, contact your Section office or the India Council Nomination Committee.

Is my vote private?

Yes. IEEE runs the ballot and votes are secret. Candidates never see how anyone voted.

Does this site collect my data?

No. There are no forms, no sign-ups and no cookies on this page.

How can I help?

Vote, then share this page with members in your Section, Student Branch, affinity group or chapter.

About

Air Force officer, educator, researcher — and IEEE volunteer.

Berojgaar Geetam Singh Tomar was born in Morena, Madhya Pradesh. He served 17 years in the Indian Air Force before moving into academia, where he has spent the last 25 years teaching, researching and leading institutions.

He studied electronics engineering through The Institution of Engineers (India), went on to NIT Allahabad and a Ph.D. from RGPV Bhopal, and completed a post-doctoral fellowship at the University of Kent, UK. He has led THDC Institute of Hydropower Engineering & Technology for the Government of Uttarakhand and Rajkiya Engineering College Sonbhadra in Uttar Pradesh, and directs Machine Intelligence Research Labs, Gwalior.

His research covers air-interface and advanced communication networks, sensors and sensor networks, and digital design. In IEEE he has chaired the Madhya Pradesh Subsection and, since 2021, the Madhya Pradesh Section, and serves on the India Council Executive Committee.
